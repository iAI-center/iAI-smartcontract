SmartChefV4 - DeFi Yield Farming Contract with Locking Mechanism

OVERVIEW:
SmartChefV4 enables users to stake tokens, withdraw funds, and earn rewards in a decentralized DeFi yield farming protocol, similar to SmartChefV1 but with a mandatory locking period mechanism.

KEY FEATURES:
1. Locking Period: Users must hold their deposits for a specified duration (e.g., 30 days) before earning rewards
2. Reward Calculation: Rewards are calculated using rewardPerBlock × number of blocks passed since earning eligibility
3. Reward Holding: During the locking period, earned rewards are held in the contract and not distributed

CORE MECHANICS:

LOCKING PERIOD RULES:
- Users can deposit at any time, even during the locking period
- Users are NOT allowed to withdraw during the locking period
- No rewards are earned during the locking period; rewards are held in the contract
- Any deposit action resets the locking period to the full duration
- Locking period countdown starts from the block of the most recent deposit

SCENARIOS (assuming 30-day lock period):

1. ADDITIONAL DEPOSIT DURING LOCK PERIOD:
   - Day 1: User deposits 100 tokens → Lock period: Day 1-30
   - Day 15: User deposits 50 tokens → New balance: 150 tokens, Lock period reset: Day 15-45
   - Rewards earned from Day 1-15: Held in contract (not claimable)
   - Rewards from Day 15+: Calculated on 150 tokens (after lock expires)

2. WITHDRAWAL DURING LOCK PERIOD:
   - Users are NOT allowed to withdraw during the locking period

3. REWARD CLAIMING AFTER LOCK EXPIRY:
   - User can claim both:
     a) Held rewards (accumulated during lock periods)
     b) Current pending rewards (earned after lock expiry)

4. WITHDRAWAL AFTER LOCK EXPIRY:
   - Full withdrawal: User receives held rewards + pending rewards + total principal
   - Partial withdrawal: User receives held rewards + pending rewards + (principal - withdrawn amount)

5. ADDITIONAL DEPOSIT AFTER LOCK EXPIRY:
   - User automatically receives held rewards + pending rewards
   - New total deposit amount becomes the basis for future reward calculations
   - New locking period begins

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Track individual user's held rewards separately from pending rewards
- Implement block-based reward calculation
- Reset locking period on deposit transactions only
- Prevent withdrawals during the locking period
- Maintain accurate reward accounting across multiple deposit/withdrawal cycles
- Locking period can only be updated by owner before the start block is reached
